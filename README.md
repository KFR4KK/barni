# creative-community-project
A community platform for creative people to showcase projects, share ideas, collaborate, and learn from each other.
Barni started as a small website for my Discord community, but the long-term vision is much bigger. I want to build a space where creative people from around the world can share their work, exchange ideas, find collaborators, and learn from each other. Whether you're a beginner looking for guidance or someone with years of experience willing to help others, Barni is meant to bring creative people together.
